Design Language · v1.0

A visual system for a credible academic institution

The foundations for Mezon Ta’lim — an Uzbek e-learning platform teaching AAOIFI Shari’ah Standards. Navy structure, gold as the single accent, warm-but-formal voice, and restrained geometry drawn from the open-book mark. Every value below maps to a CSS variable and a Tailwind token.

Colour system

Two anchors: navy carries all structure — headers, footers, headings, body links — and gold is reserved almost exclusively for the primary call-to-action and small moments of emphasis. Interface surfaces stay light. Tints and shades below are derived from the two logo colours.

Usage rules

  • •Gold is the primary action colour only — one gold button per view. Never fill large areas with gold.
  • •Navy carries structure: header, footer, section anchors, headings, and links.
  • •Surfaces are white on a --paper tint. Reserve --navy-50/100 for quiet zones.
  • •Semantic colours only for status — never decoratively.

02

Typography

Both faces carry full Cyrillic and Latin-Extended so Uzbek Latin (oʻ, gʻ, sh, ch) and Russian render natively — the reason display serifs like Fraunces were rejected.

Headings · Spectral
Shariʼat standartlari
Шариатские стандарты · oʻqish, gʻoya, mashq · A refined literary serif built for screens, with genuine Cyrillic. Warm and authoritative without feeling antique.

Практичные тесты и экзамены. A geometric-humanist sans with a tall x-height that stays crisp at small UI sizes and reads calmly in longer passages. Full Cyrillic support.

Spacing & layout

4 px base unit. All spacing, sizing, and radii are multiples of 4 for a predictable vertical rhythm. A 12-column grid at 24 px gutters; content maxes at 1200 px, prose at 720 px.

05

Iconography & the signature motif

Icons are line icons, 1.75 px stroke, 24 px grid, round joins (Lucide-style) in navy. The signature motif is a restrained diamond lattice — a modern nod to girih tessellation and to the two pages of the open-book mark. It appears only as low-opacity texture on navy fields, dividers, and the certificate border. Never loud, never full-colour.

Diamond lattice
texture on navy · 12–16% opacity

Where it appears

  • •Hero & footer navy fields, as faint watermark texture.
  • •Section dividers — a single thin lattice band.
  • •Certificate border, at low opacity in navy + gold.
  • •Empty states & auth pages, as a quiet backdrop.

06

Imagery & motion

real photo → navy duotone + gold edge

Photography treatment

Client-supplied photos of people, classrooms and offices are unified with a soft navy duotone and a subtle gold accent (a corner rule or thin baseline). Warm, dignified, never stocky. On cards, a gentle navy gradient sits under overlaid text for contrast.

Motion principles

Duration150–250ms
Easingcubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)
Entrancefade + 8px rise
Hover lifttranslateY(-2px)

Subtle and professional — short fades and slides, never bounce or spring. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
