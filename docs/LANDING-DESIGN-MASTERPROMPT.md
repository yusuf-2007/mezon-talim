ROLE
You are a senior brand + product designer AND conversion copywriter. Design the flagship
LANDING PAGE for an Uzbek professional-education platform. Deliver a distinctive,
premium, high-trust page — not a generic template. Avoid the default "AI website" look
(centered hero + three flat cards + gray shadows). Restraint plus real editorial
hierarchy. This page must sell a serious, expensive professional certification course to
busy adult banking professionals.

=====================================================================
1. THE BUSINESS — read carefully, the positioning is subtle
=====================================================================
"Mezon Ta'lim" is an Islamic-finance training center in Uzbekistan. It teaches Islamic
finance in UZBEK — practical, not dry theory — and prepares students for AAOIFI's
international certifications.

CRITICAL — the official status chain. Use this EXACT formulation everywhere, never a
shortened version:
  "Mezon Ta'lim — AAOIFI ning O'zbekistondagi rasmiy vakili bo'lgan Mezon Kengashining
   ta'lim markazi."
(= Mezon Ta'lim is the education center of Mezon Kengashi, which is AAOIFI's official
representative in Uzbekistan.) Shortening this to "Mezon Ta'lim is AAOIFI's official
representative" is FORBIDDEN — it falsely implies direct AAOIFI accreditation.

The value chain to communicate: AAOIFI (the standard) → Mezon Kengashi (practice: audits,
Shari'ah board) → Mezon Ta'lim (knowledge). Competitors can buy and read the standard;
they have not applied it in Uzbekistan. Our examples come from delivered projects, not
textbooks.

Positioning line: "Mezon — O'zbekistonda AAOIFI standartining rasmiy ta'lim markazi."
Working slogan candidate: "Standart — xalqaro. Til — o'zimizniki."
(International standard. Our own language.) — use as a hero support line.

=====================================================================
2. AUDIENCE
=====================================================================
Primary: bank and finance professionals — working, busy, demanding adults. 100+ students
in the first CPSS cohort. Secondary: entrepreneurs (for the BIM course), employers
sending staff (banks, Islamic "windows"), students, lawyers, researchers.

Their motivations: entrepreneur — "is my money halal?" → certainty and peace of mind;
professional — recognized status in a fast-growing market → career and salary. In one
line: the ability to act with confidence.

Their obstacles: materials exist only in English/Arabic; no systematic path; no time
(they work); they don't know whom to trust.

=====================================================================
3. MARKET PROOF (use as a credibility/stats block)
=====================================================================
- Uzbekistan's Law on Islamic Finance: signed 27.03.2026, in force 29.06.2026 — it
  requires banks to comply with AAOIFI standards.
- An Islamic Finance Council is being established under the Central Bank.
- Islamic finance portfolio: ~$7M in 2025 → ~$32M in the first 5 months of 2026 (4×+).
- Roadmap: at least one Islamic "window" by end-2026; independent Islamic banks by 2030.
- Acute talent shortage: banks are opening windows with no staff who understand
  Shari'ah-compliant products.
Design these as a confident data strip — this is the "why now" argument.

=====================================================================
4. THE COURSES
=====================================================================
A) AAOIFI CPSS tayyorlov kursi (flagship — the page's main subject)
   - 30 lessons / 15 weeks / 2 lessons per week / 3 hours each ≈ 90 hours
   - 30% offline, 70% online — designed for working people
   - Taught in Uzbek. Covers AAOIFI's 58 Shari'ah standards.
   - Includes: curator support (5 curators, ~20 students each), weekly quizzes,
     mock exams, bilingual terminology glossary (planned)
   - Outcomes: systematic CPSS exam preparation; ability to assess a product/contract's
     Shari'ah compliance; work in a bank's Islamic window; genuinely understand Islamic
     contracts (murabaha, ijara, mudaraba, etc.)
B) Biznesda Islom moliyasi (BIM) — for entrepreneurs, 12 lessons, 1.5–2 months,
   no prior knowledge required. Already run several cohorts (BIM-5 = fifth cohort).

HONESTY REQUIREMENT (turn into a selling point, never hide):
The AAOIFI exam itself is currently only in English and Arabic. Lessons are in Uzbek.
Present this openly AND sell the solution: a built-in "language bridge" — bilingual
terminology, practice with English-language question formulations. Design a dedicated
section for this; it converts the single biggest objection into a differentiator.

=====================================================================
5. THE CERTIFICATE — two documents, visually separated
=====================================================================
Exact site formulation:
  "CPSS sertifikatini AAOIFI beradi — o'z imtihoni orqali. Mezon Ta'lim sizni shu
   imtihonga tayyorlaydi. Kursni tamomlaganlarga Mezon Ta'limning kursni yakunlaganlik
   sertifikati beriladi."
Design these as two clearly distinct artifacts (AAOIFI's official certificate vs Mezon's
completion certificate). Do NOT blur them.

  segment; banks send staff. Include corporate invoicing/group enrollment.
Design a lead form that feels premium and low-friction (name, phone, workplace).

9. FAQ (real objections — use these, answers already approved)

10. ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS (legal/reputational — never violate)
=====================================================================

✗ Never state a date for a Uzbek-language AAOIFI exam (e.g. April 2027).
✗ Never present Mezon's certificate as AAOIFI's certificate.
✗ Never imply direct AAOIFI accreditation ("AAOIFI tomonidan akkreditatsiyalangan dastur").
✗ Never name or criticize competitors.
✗ No fatwa-level religious rulings — that is Mezon Kengashi's authority, not marketing's.
✗ No "become an expert in 3 days" energy — our entire value is seriousness.

=====================================================================
11. BRAND & VISUAL DIRECTION
=====================================================================
- Logo: open-book mark — left page NAVY, right page GOLD; wordmark "MEZON" (navy) +
  "TA'LIM" (gold). SVGs attached. Let the open-book / two-pages idea inform a subtle motif.
- Navy #023A69 (dark #033668). Gold #F8B801 (#F0B720). LIGHT interface (near-white bg),
  navy structure, gold used sparingly for primary actions only.
- Signature pattern: navy hero band with a gold primary CTA, light content below.
- Feel: ishonchli, professional, amaliy (trustworthy, professional, practical) — also
  nufuzli, aniq, halol (prestigious, precise, honest).
  NOT: arzon, oson, tez (cheap, easy, fast).
- Islamic motifs: MINIMAL and modern — restrained geometric texture at low opacity for
  dividers/certificate borders only. No heavy ornament, no calligraphy as decoration.
- NO Arabic script in the UI.
- Typography MUST support Uzbek Latin (oʻ, gʻ, sh, ch) AND Cyrillic (Russian coming).
  Do NOT use display serifs lacking Cyrillic (e.g. Fraunces). Safe: headings — Lora /
  Spectral / Source Serif 4 / PT Serif; body — Inter / Manrope. Choose and justify.
- Motion: subtle, professional. Real photography (pending) — define an imagery treatment
  (e.g. navy duotone) so mixed photos cohere.
- WCAG AA contrast: gold backgrounds require navy/dark text, never white.
- Must be implementable in Next.js + Tailwind + shadcn/ui → express everything as design
  TOKENS (CSS variables + a Tailwind theme snippet).

=====================================================================
12. SUGGESTED PAGE STRUCTURE (improve it if you can justify why)
=====================================================================
1. Sticky header: logo, nav (Kurslar, AAOIFI haqida, Ustozlar, Savol-javob), UZ/RU
